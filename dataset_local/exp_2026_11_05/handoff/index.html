  • Join labels using (sample_id, step). Both hidden and latent are supplied.
  • latent is the current CFG clean prediction, shape [C,T,H,W]=[16,5,60,104].

  • step counts model evaluations from 1; it is not the diffusion timestep.
  • raw_yuv is decoded from the corresponding clean_latent; step 20 raw is not separately decoded from final_latent.pt.
  • YUV: planar 8-bit YUV420p, 832x480, 17 frames, 16fps, BT709 limited, no header; 10183680 bytes.
  • Compression oracle labels are not included.
